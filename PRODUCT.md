# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (plain files; no framework)

## Users

Casual browser players killing a minute — short, low-stakes sessions with no account or tutorial needed.

## Product Purpose

**Get to 1 second** is a one-button timing game. A millisecond timer runs while the player holds; release (mouse up or key up) freezes the clock. Success means stopping as close to exactly 1.000s as possible; a perfect millisecond hit celebrates with confetti.

## Positioning

A single hold-to-run, release-to-stop control with millisecond precision — no menus, modes, or secondary actions. The whole game is one button and one number.

## Operating Context

Played in a desktop or mobile browser. Input is any mouse button hold/release or any keyboard key hold/release on the single control. Sessions are instant: open the page, press, release, try again.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Title: "Get to 1 second"
- On-screen millisecond timer that advances while held
- One primary control; stop on mouseup or keyup
- Exact 1.000s (to the millisecond) triggers confetti
- No secondary buttons or complex flows
- Keep purposeful motion (do not strip animation for reduced-motion unless required later)

## Brand Commitments

Name: Get to 1 second. Voice: playful and minimal — clean, no named brand system or locked visual identity. Styling is free as long as it stays simple and readable.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No logos, screenshots, or marketing copy beyond the product name and rules above. Do not fabricate testimonials or social proof.

## Product Principles

1. One control, zero confusion — the player cannot pick the wrong button.
2. Instant play — no setup, no tutorial, no friction before the first attempt.
3. Precision is the joke and the prize — milliseconds matter; celebration is reserved for exact 1.000s.
4. Playful restraint — clean and light, not loud UI chrome.
5. Motion stays — timing feedback and win celebration are part of the product feel.
